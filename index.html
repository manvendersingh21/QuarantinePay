The trust layer that lets AI agents safely spend money.

A buyer agent negotiates and proposes a machine-readable contract. The buyer approves its exact hash. PayPal holds the money, and it is captured only when deterministic policy verifies the delivery. Prompt-injected deliveries can't talk their way into a payment.

What the AI can and cannot do

Plans and negotiates

A local model extracts budget and scope; the buyer's own written amount is a hard cap. Seller agents negotiate within budget and floors.

Proposes, never signs

Only the buyer's approval of the exact contract hash creates a signed intent. Any change to the terms is refused.

Adds caution, never removes it

The AI analyst can flag manipulation or recommend rejection, but it can't lift a policy hold or move money.

Honest outcomes

Uncertain or refused provider results become reconciliation_required, never a claimed success.
