QQuarantinePay
PAYPAL AI HACKATHON · AGENTIC COMMERCE

An AI agent that buys on its own — and can’t be tricked into paying.

Sign one spending mandate. The buyer agent then discovers real products (live via Channel3) or service sellers, negotiates, signs a contract, and authorizes payment through your saved PayPal wallet, with no approval clicks. Money is captured only when deterministic checks and an AI analyst independently verify the delivery. Prompt-injected deliveries can't talk their way into a payment.

Buying a real product

A live Channel3 search finds real LED desk lamps, and the agent buys one through a reseller agent with no click. Payment is captured only after a carrier-signed delivery proof; a forged proof is voided. Listings and prices are real; resellers, fulfilment and the carrier are simulated.

Buying from a real Shopify merchant

The agent searches a live Shopify store first. It withholds a listing whose description tries to instruct it, and buys the lamp at the store's own price with no click. PayPal authorizes before the Shopify order exists. A seller's "delivered" claim is refused. Payment is captured only when QuarantinePay's own read of the merchant's Shopify order shows the exact item fulfilled with tracking to the buyer. Then Shopify is updated to match PayPal. This runs on a development store with test orders; the fulfilment is the merchant's own record, not carrier proof.

What the AI can and cannot do

Plans and negotiates

A local model extracts budget and scope; the buyer's own written amount is a hard cap. Seller agents negotiate within budget and floors.

Acts only inside a signed mandate

Autonomous purchases must pass a deterministic mandate check (signature, cap, budget, seller, scope) under a lock. Anything outside it goes back to the buyer.

Adds caution, never removes it

The AI analyst can flag manipulation or recommend rejection, but it can't lift a policy hold or move money.

Honest outcomes

Uncertain or refused provider results become reconciliation_required, never a claimed success.

Boundaries

Demo honesty: sellers, reports and deliveries are fictional. Payments are PayPal Sandbox test money, and a hold is not escrow. Checks verify structure and counts, not truth. Injection detection can't guarantee it catches everything, which is why it can only add caution. The video's voice-over is synthesized, and waits for the model and PayPal are cut.
